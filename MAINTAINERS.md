## The Duties of a Maintainer

The Maintainers are collectively responsible for ensuring the following duties are fulfilled. Not every Maintainer is expected to perform every duty; rather, the maintainer body as a whole is accountable.

- Review, respond to, and act on security vulnerabilities in accordance with `SECURITY.md`
- Review, provide feedback on, and merge or reject pull requests
- Review, triage, comment on, and close GitHub issues
- Coordinate the technical direction of the Project
- Lead or facilitate architectural discussions where appropriate
- Lead or facilitate the creation of a product roadmap where appropriate
- Create, clarify, and label issues for Contributors
- Ensure a well-defined (ideally automated) test and release pipeline
- Execute the product release process where appropriate
- Maintain `CONTRIBUTING.md` and onboarding documentation
- Contribute via pull requests
- Monitor LFDT TAC requests regarding repository standards
- Contribute to the LFDT Project Quarterly Report

## Becoming a Maintainer

Linea welcomes community contributions. Contributors may progress to become Maintainers.

### Requirements

To be eligible:
- At least **5 significant code contributions** (merged PRs) to the repository

### Approval Process

- A Contributor is approved as a Maintainer by **majority approval (51%)** of Maintainers (TSC)
- The candidate must be sponsored by at least one Maintainer
- A PR is created proposing the addition
- The candidate must confirm acceptance and intent to contribute long-term (>6 months)
- The proposal is communicated across community channels

### Voting

- Each Maintainer has one vote
- Approval requires **51% majority**
- Voting period: **2 weeks**
- If no majority is reached, the proposal may be closed

---

## Removing Maintainers

Maintainer status is not permanent and may be updated when appropriate.

### Reasons for Removal

- Voluntary resignation
- Code of Conduct violations
- Inactivity (typically no activity for two reporting quarters)
- Other justified circumstances

Exceptions may be made for known long-term leave.

### Removal Process

- A PR is created to move the Maintainer to emeritus status
- Voluntary resignations: merge after one approval
- Other removals: require **51% majority approval within 2 weeks**
- Communication occurs across relevant channels

---

## Returning from Emeritus

Returning to active status follows the same process as adding a new Maintainer.
